Tuning & policy

  1. CAMAL: Optimizing LSM-trees via Active Learning (Yu, Luo, Yu, Cong), SIGMOD 2025 (2409.15130). Active-learning tuner combining cost models, decoupled per-parameter analysis, and incremental updates; in RocksDB, "28% on average and up to 8x" over SOTA configs.
  1. Rethinking the Compaction Policies in LSM-trees (EcoTune; Wang, Qiu, Yuan, Zhang), SIGMOD 2025 (search) (PDF). Treats compaction as an investment in future query throughput; dynamic programming finds the optimal policy; 1.5–3× query throughput vs leveling, up to 2.5× vs lazy leveling.
  2. How to Grow an LSM-tree? (Vertiorizon; Mo, Luo, Idreos), SIGMOD 2025 (2504.17178). Hybrid of vertical growth (add levels, as RocksDB/LevelDB) and horizontal growth (bigger level capacities); better writes than vertical, ~6× less extra space than horizontal.
  3. ArceKV (Liu, Xie, Luo), arXiv Aug 2025, rev. Jan 2026, no venue stated (2508.03565). ElasticLSM relaxes structural constraints (adaptive compactions and write stalls as actions); a lightweight decision engine picks actions; "around 3x faster" in dynamic workloads. The paper's Fig. 5 draws a "stable tree state" as a set of runs across three levels, each with a Bloom filter, and counts them as s = |S| = 6.
  1. AXE: Task Decomposition Approach to Learned LSM Tuning (Huynh, Saha, Chaudhari, Athanassoulis), PVLDB 18(13) 2025 (search) (PDF). Learned cost model as loss for a learned tuner; ~100× lower tuning overhead.

Stalls, offload, disaggregation

  1. DiaLSM: Write-Stall-Free Performance via Shard-based LSM-tree (Byun et al.), "Accepted to ICDE 2027" per its page, submitted Sept 2026 (2609.14370). Queuing argument that a monolithic write-flush-compaction pipeline cannot eliminate stalls; internal sharding with fallback redirection; 2.4× throughput, 94% fewer stalls vs ADOC/sub-compaction on RocksDB.
  2. RESYSTANCE (Byun, Lee, Yoo, Kim, Park), ICDE 2026 (2603.05162). Moves compaction reads into the kernel via eBPF + io_uring, no change to the algorithm: 99% fewer syscalls, 50% faster compaction, 75% higher write-heavy throughput, 40% lower p99.
  3. CaaS-LSM: Compaction-as-a-Service (Yu, Guo, Zhuang, Thakkar, Wang, Cao), SIGMOD 2024 (search) (ACM). Stateless compaction service decoupled from the LSM instance; up to 8× throughput, up to 98% lower p99 vs conventional LSM-KVS (RocksDB prototype, Kvrocks and Nebula).
  4. O³-LSM (Lin et al.), SIGMOD 2026 (2603.05439). Extends compaction offload to memtable and flush offload over disaggregated memory; up to 4.5× write throughput, 76% lower p99.
  5. SlateDB (object-storage LSM): pluggable scheduler (only size-tiered shipped), distributed compaction via shared .compactions file, subcompactions on the roadmap; scaling numbers are the project's own claims (design, roadmap). Not a paper.

Key-value separation

  1. Scavenger: Space-Time Trade-offs for KV-Separated LSM-trees (Zhang et al.), ICDE 2024; arXiv upload Aug 2025 (2508.13909). I/O-efficient GC plus space-aware compaction (compensated size); better than BlobDB, Titan, TerarkDB. Extension Scavenger+ in IEEE TC 2025 (2508.13935). Venue discrepancy resolved: late arXiv upload of an ICDE 2024 paper (IEEE Xplore record seen via search; dblp not reachable).

Survey

  1. Rethinking LSM-tree based Key-Value Stores: A Survey (Lv et al.), arXiv Jul 2025, no venue (2507.09642). Five years of work on compaction, stalls, and read amplification (100+ papers per search snippet).

Adjacent, fetched but not compaction-policy: Mnemosyne (SIGMOD 2025, Bloom-filter memory tuning), dLSM (VLDB Journal 2024, disaggregated memory), LSM-OPD (2508.11862, scans on compressed data), Nezha (2603.09122, ICDE 2026, KV separation + Raft).

Pattern: compaction is becoming adaptive/learned (CAMAL, EcoTune, ArceKV, AXE), stalls are attacked structurally (DiaLSM), compaction cost by kernel and offload tricks (RESYSTANCE, CaaS-LSM, O³-LSM), and KV-separation co-designs GC with compaction (Scavenger).

Still unverified (search snippets only, not cited above): D2Comp and DFlush (DPU offload), multi-threaded compaction pipelining, GPComp/gLSM (GPU), the EDBT 2025 write-amplification benchmark paper. No ZNS-specific compaction paper found.
