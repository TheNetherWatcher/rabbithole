kv_alloc_sim.py (in this folder) simulates a 32,768-slot pool with max_len 2048 and 3000 synthetic lognormal requests under FCFS admission.

python3 kv_alloc_sim.py

  • Contiguous/max at 32% falls inside the paper's 20.4–38.2% band.
  • Oracle reaches only 50%: 79% of its free memory sits outside the largest hole.
  • Paged/16 at 96.9% matches the paper's 96.3%, with three times the concurrency.
  • Utilization falls as B grows, per (B−1)/(2E[ℓ]). Preemptions are the price of greedy admission; add a watermark and watch them drop.
