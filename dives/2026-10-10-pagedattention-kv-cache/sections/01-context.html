• Autoregressive decoding. One token per forward pass; each attends to all previous tokens. (Vaswani 2017)
  • KV cache. Per-layer key/value vectors of past tokens, cached so they aren't recomputed. Grows one row per token. (HF docs)
  • Prefill vs decode. Prefill is the whole prompt in parallel and compute-bound. Decode is one token at a time and memory-bandwidth-bound, so throughput comes from batching. (DistServe)
  • Continuous batching. Requests join and leave the batch at every step. (Orca, OSDI'22)
  • OS virtual memory. Pages, page tables, copy-on-write fork(), swapping. (OSTEP)
  • GQA. Several query heads share one K/V head, which shrinks the KV cache. (Ainslie 2023)
