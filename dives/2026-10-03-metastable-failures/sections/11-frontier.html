1. Analyzing Metastable Failures: Isaacs, Alvaro, Majumdar, Muniswamy-Reddy, Salamati, Soudjani (AWS / UCSC / MPI-SWS). HotOS 2025. A Python DSL (servers, queues, thread pools, timeouts, retry policies) feeds a ladder of tools: CTMC (ms–s), discrete-event simulation (minutes), emulator on the production RPC stack (hours), stress test. The abstract tools say where to look and the concrete ones confirm. Being applied to AWS control planes to find tipping points. PDF · code: metafor
  2. Formal Analysis of Metastable Failures in Software Systems: same group. arXiv 2510.03551, Oct 2025 (no peer-reviewed venue found). Defines metastability through escape probabilities and the generator's eigenstructure, and gives algorithms to predict recovery time. arXiv
  3. Learning Metastable Dynamics: Majumdar, Salamati, Singh, Soudjani. arXiv 2609.14712, Sep 2026 (HSCC'26 per arXiv metadata). Learns a Koopman embedding (a latent space where the dynamics are linear) from observed trajectories and reads metastability off the dominant eigenvalues. Flags it after ~10% of the simulated run. Evaluated on simulations only. arXiv
  4. Characterizing Metastable Faults and Failures: Farahbakhsh, Lu, Alvisi, Haeberlen, van Renesse (Cornell/Penn). arXiv 2606.00942, May 2026 (under submission). Recasts metastability as "sins of composition": a cycle of components, each of whose locally stabilising action destabilises the next. Covers oscillating failures (cluster membership flapping at a large gaming platform) that the trigger/loop taxonomy misses. arXiv
  5. CSnake: Qian, Tan, Zhang. EuroSys 2026. Finds self-sustaining cascading failures before deployment by stitching single-fault injections from different tests into causal chains and searching for cycles. 15 bugs in 5 widely deployed systems, 5 confirmed. arXiv
  6. Wasabi ("If At First You Don't Succeed, Try, Try, Again…?"): Stoica, Sethi, Su, Zhou, Lu, Mace, Musuvathi, Nath. SOSP 2024. Fault injection at retry sites plus CodeQL + GPT-4 static checks. More than 100 new retry bugs (IF/WHEN/HOW to retry) in 8 Java applications. PDF
  7. Atropos: Hu et al. SOSP 2025. Overload often comes from one culprit task hogging a lock or buffer pool, not from total demand. Atropos tracks per-task resource usage and cancels the highest-gain task through the application's own cancellation hooks. Integrated with MySQL, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch and etcd; keeps 96% throughput with p99 within 1.16× of normal and drops < 0.01% of requests. PDF
  8. Rajomon: Xing et al. NSDI 2025. Market-based overload control for microservices: tokens on requests, per-API prices piggybacked through the call graph. 78% lower tail latency and 45% higher goodput than prior work. USENIX

Also: HoliPaxos (PVLDB 2025, Charapko et al.) folds failure and slowdown detection into MultiPaxos itself to avoid bolt-on metastability. MSF-Model (SRDS 2024, UCI/Roblox) is a queueing model for replicated storage. RetryGuard (arXiv 2511.23278) does graph-wide retry-policy control and reports 98% smaller storms than AWS default policies.
