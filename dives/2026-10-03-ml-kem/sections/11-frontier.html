1. KyberSlash: Bernstein, Bhargavan, Bhasin, Chattopadhyay, Chia, Kannwischer, Kiefer, Paiva, Ravi, Tamvada. TCHES 2025(2), CHES 2025 Best Paper. Secret-dependent division timing in compress; practical key recovery; Valgrind patch scanning more than 1000 SUPERCOP implementations. ePrint 2024/1049
  2. Formally Verifying Kyber Episode V: Almeida et al. CRYPTO 2024. Machine-checked IND-CCA security and correctness of ML-KEM in EasyCrypt, linked to a verified constant-time Jasmin implementation. ePrint 2024/843
  3. Keeping Up with the KEMs: Cremers, Dax, Medinger. ACM CCS 2024. Defines the X-BIND-P-Q binding notions, analyses KEM-based protocols automatically in Tamarin, and shows implicit rejection complicates MAL-binding. ePrint 2023/1933. Companion: Unbindable Kemmy Schmidt, Schmieg 2024: ML-KEM is neither MAL-BIND-K-CT nor MAL-BIND-K-PK. ePrint 2024/523
  4. X-Wing: Barbosa, Connolly, Duarte, Kaiser, Schwabe, Varner, Westerbaan. IACR CiC 1(1), 2024. Hybrid KEM K = SHA3-256(label ‖ ss_M ‖ ss_X ‖ ct_X ‖ pk_X). It can leave ct_M and pk_M out of the hash because ML-KEM's binding of honest keys is proven. Shipped in Apple CryptoKit HPKE. ePrint 2024/039
  5. Triple Ratchet / SPQR: Dodis, Jost, Katsumata, Prest, Schmidt (and Auerbach). Eurocrypt 2025 + USENIX Security 2025. Bandwidth-efficient hybrid-secure Signal with an ML-KEM Braid and erasure-coded chunks. Deployed Oct 2025. ePrint 2025/078
  6. Dual attacks on firmer ground: Ducas–Pulles, "Does the Dual-Sieve Attack on LWE even Work?" (CRYPTO 2023, 2023/302) showed MATZOV-style estimates relied on a flawed independence heuristic. Since then: Pouly–Shen, provable dual attacks (EUROCRYPT 2024, 2023/1508); Ducas–Pulles accurate score prediction (J. Cryptology 2026, 2023/1850); a provable FFT dual attack on ML-KEM (2026/2166; seen in search results only). No result puts ML-KEM below its claimed category.
  7. Side channels against protected code: chosen-ciphertext attacks on shuffled Kyber, about 1,072 queries (2025/1956); SPA on the KyberSlash-patched code (2024/2051); an open EM dataset of 200k traces each for the reference, pqm4 and masked implementations (2026/1851).
