Alternatives:

  • CFS: minimum vruntime plus heuristics. Easier to reason about in isolation, but latency is controlled by tuning.
  • BFS, MuQSS, BORE: burst-score heuristics, favoured on some desktop kernels.
  • SCHED_DEADLINE: hard EDF plus CBS reservations.
  • Stride and lottery scheduling: O(1) or probabilistic, with no latency input.
  • BPF sched_ext: an arbitrary policy.

EEVDF won mainline because it has one theory-backed rule and removes tunables.
