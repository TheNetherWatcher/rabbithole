Two people edit one shared note, offline, on separate flights.

No server decides who goes first. When the planes land, both copies must merge into the same text, and that text must still read sensibly. Algorithms that do this are sequence CRDTs. On one real two-author trace, the textbook algorithm needed 61 minutes to merge.
