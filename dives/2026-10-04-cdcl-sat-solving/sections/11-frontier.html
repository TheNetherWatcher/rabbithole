Three open-licensed figures from the two 2026 papers above, placed where they support the claim. The first two come from the graph-backtracking paper: propagation counts per backtracking variant, then a cactus plot of time against instances solved.

The propagation boxplots show graph backtracking doing fewer propagations than NCB and CB across the 3-colouring set, with and without Luby restarts. The median drops, and so does the upper quartile.

Solve time is a different story. Plain graph backtracking trails NCB at the easy end of the curve, and the ECM variant recovers the lead in the tail. So the propagation saving does not translate into a uniform speedup.

The proofdoor study's exponential family is the other side of the frontier: solving time climbs steeply once the clause count passes about 2.5 million.
