tREFI = tREFW / 8192 = 64 ms / 8192 = 7.8 µs
max ACT per window ≈ tREFW / tRC = 64 ms / 47 ns ≈ 1.36M (derived)→ in plain terms: the attacker's whole budget is about 1.36M hammers. A defense only has to make "aim 4.8K of them at one victim, undetected" impossible.

The 8192 REFs per window (Kim et al. 2014 §2.3) give the refresh interval. The activation ceiling is derived from the timing parameters, not measured.

PARA: the stateless probabilistic defense

On every row close, with probability p the controller refreshes an adjacent row. It keeps no counters and no addresses. The chance that a specific victim is never rescued over N aggressor activations is:

Pmiss = (1 − p)N (or (1 − p/2)N if the coin picks one of two neighbours)→ in plain terms: each hammer is an independent coin flip for "refresh your neighbour now." To dodge rescue for N hammers, every coin has to come up tails, and that probability decays geometrically. With p ≈ 0.001–0.005, Pmiss over a window falls below the hardware hard-error budget of roughly 10⁻¹⁵ at under 0.75% performance cost.

Why this form and not a counter? It needs zero state, and that is the whole point. Its weakness is also in the equation. Pmiss depends on N, the number of hammers you must survive. As HC_first falls, you must raise p, and the overhead climbs.

Graphene: counting with Misra–Gries

To refresh exactly the hot rows, you must identify every row that exceeds a threshold within a window, and do it cheaply. Misra–Gries heavy hitters need only k−1 counters to find every element that occurs more than N/k times in a stream of N items, and each stored estimate never exceeds the true count, so there are no false negatives. Applied to Rowhammer, to catch any row that reaches about T_RH/2 activations among N_ACT activations in a window:

entries > N_ACT / (T_RH / 2) = 2 · N_ACT / T_RH→ in plain terms: you don't need a counter per row. You need one per "slot of hammering budget big enough to matter", which is about 2·N_ACT/T_RH. Storage drops from billions of rows to a few hundred entries per bank, but it still scales inversely with T_RH. As the threshold falls from 1000 to 125, storage balloons from about 207 KiB to about 1.5 MiB per the CoMeT analysis.
