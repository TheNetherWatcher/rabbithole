The leaky-bucket grid with a patrolling top-up truck. Each cell is a bucket slowly leaking. The refresh truck tops every bucket up every 64 ms. Hammering a row shakes its neighbours' buckets so they leak faster. A flip happens when a neighbour empties past half before the truck arrives. TRR is "a spotter riding the truck who notes which buckets you've been shaking a lot and tops those neighbours up early." PRAC is "a counter bolted to every bucket that rings a bell the moment it has been shaken too much."

Where the analogy breaks. The disturbance isn't mechanical vibration. It is three electrical effects: coupling, bridges, and hot-carrier injection. It can reach the bucket two rows away (Half-Double). It can also be driven by holding a bucket open rather than shaking it (RowPress). A mechanical-vibration intuition predicts neither.
