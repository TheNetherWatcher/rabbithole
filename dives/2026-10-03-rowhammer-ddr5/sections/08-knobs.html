Why did counting win the design argument? Sampling was repeatedly and structurally broken. Why hasn't it simply ended the problem? Exact per-row counting at DRAM scale is expensive in area, latency, and energy. The whole 2024–26 PRAC-optimisation literature exists to claw that cost back.
