Core insight: programs are far more regular than the language permits. Real call sites see one shape more than 90% of the time. The Self and PIC measurements and decades of JS engine telemetry back this. I did not find a single canonical V8 number, so treat the figure as folklore with support.

The ablations show which piece carries what:

  • No Maps: per-object name tables and hash lookups. Slow and memory-heavy.
  • Maps but no ICs: still a lookup keyed on the Map. The slow path forever.
  • ICs but no speculation (Ignition and Sparkplug): one compare and one load, but no inlining, no escape analysis, and no field-type knowledge.
  • Speculation but no deopt: unsound. Any surprise miscomputes. Deopt is the safety net that makes aggressive guards legal.
  • Speculation with a bug in dependency or guard elimination: type confusion (see the "where it breaks" card).
