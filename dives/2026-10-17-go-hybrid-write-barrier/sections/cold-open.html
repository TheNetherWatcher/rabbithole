A Go server can run half a million goroutines at once.

Each one has its own stack, and the garbage collector must read every stack to know what is still in use. Before Go 1.8 it had to read them a second time, with the whole program frozen, at the very end of every collection cycle.

Go 1.7: freeze, re-scan every dirty stack. 10s to 100s of ms
Go 1.8 hybrid barrier. Under 50 µs worst case (design-doc experiments), at least 200× shorter
