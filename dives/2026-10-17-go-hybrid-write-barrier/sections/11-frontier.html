Plain statement: the barrier design is settled. It has been the same hybrid shape since Go 1.8 (2017), and I found no 2024–2026 paper proposing a new Go write barrier (four extended searches, several dead ends). The active work moved to how the marking is ordered and how the pacer reasons, plus neighboring runtimes' barriers.
