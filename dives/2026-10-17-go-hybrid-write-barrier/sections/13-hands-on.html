Experiment 2 · Watch STW stay flat as goroutines grow (5 min)

go run gcdemo.go -g 1000
go run gcdemo.go -g 100000
go run gcdemo.go -g 500000
GODEBUG=gctrace=1 go run gcdemo.go -g 100000 2>&1 | grep '^gc ' | head

My run (Go 1.27.2, 14 P, Apple silicon, 3 s of pointer churn):

goroutines=1000   GCs=9 max STW pause=62us  total STW=0.4ms
goroutines=100000 GCs=9 max STW pause=133us total STW=0.4ms
goroutines=500000 GCs=9 max STW pause=104us total STW=0.4ms

The worst pause stays around 100 µs from 1,000 to 500,000 goroutines. That is the headline of the design doc, observed. Caveat: the goroutines are idle and the heap is small, so this shows the absence of a goroutine-count-scaling STW, not that your service is pause-free. Also try GOGC=25 and GOGC=400 and watch the cycle count and the GCCPUFraction printed at the end.
