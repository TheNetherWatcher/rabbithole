ApproachNeedsUsed by
Insertion only (Dijkstra)STW rescan of stacks (or barriers on stacks)Go 1.5–1.7
Deletion only (Yuasa / SATB)A consistent snapshot, so all stacks scanned atomically at the startShenandoah's marking barrier, G1 (SATB)
Hybrid, conditionalKnow whether the current stack is greyDesign doc's proof shape
Hybrid, unconditionalNothing; ~2× the cost of the conditional form for most of the mark phaseWhat Go's buffer effectively does
Load barrier (colored pointers)Cost on every pointer load, pointer metadataZGC

Go bets that stores are rarer than loads, that a non-moving non-generational collector is simple enough to keep latency low, and that the barrier is off most of the time. The same bet fails for programs that mutate pointers heavily during long marks.
