A river with a stone bridge being built: LMD-GHOST is the floating raft you steer toward the strongest current (most recent votes); FFG periodically drives a stone pier (checkpoint) into the riverbed once two-thirds sign off. The raft may drift between piers; it can never cross a pier.

Where it breaks: the raft is steered by latest currents, so a patient adversary can manipulate which current is "latest" at the moment it matters; piers also only get built if two-thirds are paying attention.
