• Aggregate LOD. No accepted way to simplify foliage or hair into a coarser representation that preserves coverage and shading. Voxels are Epic's first production answer (UE 5.8), and they are still new.
  • Deforming geometry. The bounds and the DAG assume rigid shapes, and skinning, WPO and morphs break the error bounds. Skinned Nanite is marked experimental in UE 5.5+ (secondary). It is hard because the error bound must hold for every pose.
  • Ray tracing. Cluster BVHs are rebuilt per frame at about 1 px LOD. Open question: how engines keep LOD consistent between primary and secondary rays, and how they stream and blend LOD for them.
  • Portability. 64-bit image atomics and persistent threads limit Nanite to desktop-class GPUs. Mobile and WebGPU ports (community, such as Scthe's nanite-webgpu) are partial (secondary).
  • Attribute-aware error. QEM weights normals and UVs heuristically, with no perceptual error model tied to shading.
  • Splats vs meshes. A 3DGS splat has no shared border to lock, so seams are hidden by blending (LODGE). A principled analogue is unsettled.
