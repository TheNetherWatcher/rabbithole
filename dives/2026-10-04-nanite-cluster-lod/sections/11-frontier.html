CuRast (verified)Apr 2026 · arXivCUDA software rasterizer with a 3-stage pipeline chosen by triangle size. It is 2–5× faster than Vulkan on unique geometry and up to 12× on instanced geometry, on hundreds of millions of triangles. Vulkan stays about an order of magnitude faster for low-poly meshes. It targets dense opaque photogrammetry, and it shows the "software raster for dense meshes" idea is alive.
Virtualized 3D Gaussians (verified)May 2025 · arXivA Nanite-inspired cluster hierarchy for 3D Gaussian splats: an offline hierarchy with online cluster selection, scaling to billions of Gaussians.
LODGE (verified)2025 · NeurIPSHierarchical LOD for large-scale Gaussian splatting, with chunked dynamic loading. It hides seams with opacity blending, because splats have no shared vertices to lock.
RTX Mega Geometry (verified)Feb 2025 · NVIDIACluster and partitioned acceleration structures for Vulkan (VK_NV_cluster_acceleration_structure), with open cluster and LOD-cluster builders, and continuous-LOD streaming for ray tracing.
Nanite Foliage (verified)UE 5.8 docsAssemblies (instanced parts), Voxels (near-pixel-sized voxels at distance) and Skinning (bone-driven wind). The doc's example is a 41M-triangle tree with 12 unique parts, 2,160 instances and 850 bones. Disk falls from 3.5 GB to 29 MB for the largest tree, and streaming memory per tree in view falls from 36 MB to 2.7 MB.
