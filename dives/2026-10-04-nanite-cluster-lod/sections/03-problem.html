  • Source size vs screen size. A source asset has 1M–1B triangles. A 1080p screen has 2.07M pixels. The detail you can actually see is at most about one triangle per pixel, so at most roughly 2M visible triangles, or about 16k clusters, independent of source size.
  • Classic discrete LOD pops when it switches, needs authoring per asset, and costs a draw call and CPU culling per object.
  • Hardware rasterizer inefficiency. Pixels are shaded in 2×2 quads, so a triangle covering one pixel still launches at least 4 pixel-shader lanes. At least 75% of that work is thrown away as helper lanes.
  • The constraint that forces the design. LOD decisions must be local (per cluster, in parallel on the GPU), yet the result must be globally watertight.
