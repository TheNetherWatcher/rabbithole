• Cluster size: 128 triangles. It gives a 7-bit triangle ID and is a multiple of the wave size. A meshoptimizer user report found 256 vertices with 128 triangles beat 64 with 64, because rasterization cost dominated (verified).
  • Group size: 8–32 clusters (secondary). Bigger groups simplify better but couple more tightly to streaming.
  • τ ≈ 1 px. A bigger τ is faster but shimmers more. Nanite's visual stability leans on temporal AA.
  • Position precision. A 2P grid, at most 21 bits per axis, trades size against stair-stepping (secondary).
  • Streaming pages. 128 KB pages, with 32 KB root pages (secondary; not stated on the Epic technical-details page I fetched).
  • Alternatives. Classic discrete LOD (cheap, but pops and needs authoring). View-dependent progressive meshes (Hoppe), which cannot be evaluated in parallel per cluster. Quick-VDR's clustered hierarchy of progressive meshes (Yoon et al., 2004) (secondary). Mesh shaders and meshlets without a DAG. Voxels or impostors for aggregates.
