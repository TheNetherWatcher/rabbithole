• Rasterization: the GPU turns triangles into pixels; hardware shades pixels in 2×2 quads (needed for texture derivatives).
  • LOD (level of detail): cheaper versions of a mesh used when far away. Classic LOD is a few hand- or auto-made versions per object, switched by distance.
  • Meshlet / cluster: a small fixed-size triangle batch (here 128 triangles) processed as a unit.
  • Quadric error metric (QEM): Garland & Heckbert's way to collapse mesh edges while tracking geometric error (CMU).
  • Visibility buffer: write "which triangle is here" per pixel instead of shading; shade later, once per pixel.
  • HZB: hierarchical Z-buffer, a mip pyramid of the depth buffer holding the farthest depth per region; used for conservative occlusion tests.
