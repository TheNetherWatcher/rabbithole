Bloom 1970 → Carter et al. 1978 (fingerprint filters, the bound) → Bloomier filters (Chazelle et al. 2004) → Dietzfelbinger–Pagh 2008 (succinct retrieval and AMQ) → Botelho–Pagh–Ziviani 2013 (peeling for perfect hashing) → XOR filters (Graf–Lemire 2019/2020) → Dietzfelbinger–Walzer 2019 (banded elimination) → Ribbon (Dillinger–Walzer 2021) and BuRR 2022 → Walzer 2020 (spatial coupling) → binary fuse (Graf–Lemire 2022) → ZOR (2026).
