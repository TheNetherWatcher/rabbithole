• Autoregressive decoding: one forward pass of the model per output token; token t+1 depends on token t, so passes are serial. (Leviathan et al.)
  • Memory-bandwidth bound: at small batch, each decode step reads all weights + the KV cache from HBM but does very little math on them; time ≈ bytes / bandwidth, not FLOPs / peak. (Chen et al.)
  • KV cache: per-layer keys/values for every previous token, kept so each new token only computes its own row of attention. (vLLM docs)
  • Logits → distribution: temperature, top-k, top-p are transforms that turn logits into the distribution p you actually sample from.
  • Total variation distance TV(p,q) = ½ Σ|p(x) − q(x)|: the largest gap between the probabilities p and q assign to any event.
  • Teacher forcing / scoring: given a known sequence, one forward pass returns the model's next-token distribution at every position at once (same thing as a prefill).
