• cwnd (congestion window): the cap on unacknowledged bytes in flight, set by the sender. (RFC 5681)
  • Loss-based CC: Reno/CUBIC grow cwnd until a packet drops, then multiply it by β (0.5 for Reno, 0.7 for CUBIC). (RFC 9438 (CUBIC))
  • BDP (bandwidth-delay product): bottleneck rate × base RTT, the number of bytes needed to keep the pipe full. (ACM Queue BBR paper)
  • Bufferbloat: oversized FIFO buffers that loss-based CC fills, which adds hundreds of ms of queueing delay. (bufferbloat.net)
  • Pacing: spacing packets out at a target rate instead of sending a burst whenever cwnd opens. Linux does it in the fq qdisc or inside TCP. (fasterdata BBR notes)
  • ECN: routers set a CE bit instead of dropping. The receiver echoes it back. (RFC 3168)
