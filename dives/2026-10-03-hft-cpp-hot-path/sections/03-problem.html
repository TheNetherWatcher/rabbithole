Concrete constraints a designer faces:

The physics floor

  • Chicago (CME, Aurora) to New Jersey (Carteret/Secaucus): 1139.5 km geodesic, so the speed-of-light round trip is 7.6 ms.
  • Microwave networks measure 7.7 ms RTT, within 1.5% of c.
  • Market data shows CME→NJ reactions at ≈4.2 ms one-way, versus ≈6.65 ms for the best fiber.
  • cISP paper (Bhattacherjee et al.), The Register 2013

Once everyone is on the same microwave path and in the same colo, what remains is inside the box.

The box budget

"A very good minimum time (wire to wire) for a software-based trading system is around 2.5 µs" (Cook 2017). Gross 2024: strategy layer 1–10 µs, ultra-low-latency execution < 1 µs.
