1. TLS 1.2 session tickets (RFC 5077, 2008): stateless resumption, but the first request still waits a round trip.
  2. Google QUIC (designed ~2013): sent a request in the first packet by caching server config; replay was an accepted trade-off.
  3. OPTLS (Krawczyk & Wee, 2015, ePrint 2015/978): the design background for 0-RTT with forward-secrecy goals in TLS 1.3.
  4. TLS 1.3 drafts 2015–2017: a (EC)DHE-based 0-RTT mode and a PSK-based 0-RTT mode were analysed (Fischlin & Günther, 2017, draft-12 and draft-14). The final standard keeps only PSK-based 0-RTT.
  5. RFC 8446 (2018): early_data, the three anti-replay mechanisms in §8, the “clients MUST only send replay-safe early data” rule.
  6. RFC 8470 (2018): Early-Data header and 425 Too Early. RFC 9001 (2021): QUIC inherits it.
  7. 2019 → 2026: forward-secure 0-RTT constructions, then post-quantum resumption.
