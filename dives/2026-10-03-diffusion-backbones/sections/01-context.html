• Diffusion / flow matching. Training adds Gaussian noise to data, and a network learns to undo it. Sampling runs that network 4–50+ times, starting from noise. DDPM, rectified flow in SD3
  • Prediction target. The network outputs one of three things: the noise ε, the clean image x₀, or a velocity v. All three are linear functions of each other given x_t and t, but the choice affects how hard learning is (see §6 and §11).
  • Latent diffusion. A VAE first compresses the image, for example 1024²×3 down to 128²×16. Diffusion then runs in that space, and the decoder maps back to pixels. LDM
  • Self-attention costs O(T²·d) for T tokens of width d. Cross-attention uses queries from one sequence and keys and values from another. Attention
  • Conditioning. The timestep t, a class label or text, and the guidance scale all have to get into the network somehow. How they get in is a backbone design decision.
  • FID. The Fréchet distance between Inception features of real and generated images; lower is better. It is the ImageNet benchmark currency, and it has flaws (§9). CMMD critique
