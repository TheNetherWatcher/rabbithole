• Training instability at scale.
    • Attention logits grow, attention entropy collapses, and bf16 training diverges. SD3 fixed this with RMSNorm on Q and K plus AdamW ε=1e-15. The authors call it "not a universal recipe".
    • Fixing logits exposes massive activations, reported at about 15K at 256 px and 24K at 512 px in rectified-flow transformers, which overflow fp16. (2510.04236; numbers taken from the abstract/summary, not verified in full text.)
    • U-Nets have their own version: EDM2 showed weights and activations drift upward during training, which silently decays the effective learning rate.
  • Off-resolution generation duplicates subjects, for different reasons in each family.
    • U-Nets: the conv receptive field is too small at the new resolution (ScaleCrafter).
    • DiTs: position embeddings are extrapolated beyond training (FiT, LEDiT).
    • Both: a wrong timestep shift.
  • Reconstruction vs generation tradeoff in the VAE.
    • Higher-dimensional latents reconstruct better and generate worse for a fixed denoiser. For VA-VAE without alignment: f16d16 reconstructs at rFID 0.49 and generates at gFID 16.24; f16d64 reconstructs at 0.17 and generates at gFID 36.83 (VA-VAE).
    • The backbone gets blamed for a latent-space problem.
  • Text rendering and counting.
    • Spelling is mostly a tokenizer problem. Character-aware ByT5 gains 25–30+ points over T5 (Liu et al.).
    • Counting fails across all state-of-the-art models and does not improve with scale (T2ICountBench, numerosity).
    • "MMDiT fixes composition" is unproven.
  • Quadratic video.
    • HunyuanVideo, 5 s at 720p: 945 s of inference, of which 800 s is attention (STA).
    • Factorized spatial/temporal attention was abandoned for full 3D because it fails on large motion (CogVideoX).
  • Memorization.
    • More than 1,000 training images have been extracted from Stable Diffusion and Imagen (Carlini 2023).
    • DiTs lack the "geometry-adaptive harmonic bases" that explain U-Net generalization. DiT generalization correlates with attention locality (2410.21273).
    • No matched U-Net vs DiT extraction study exists.
  • The ecosystem resets. LoRAs, ControlNets and IP-Adapters are architecture-specific. FLUX.1-dev has about 42.7K adapters, and moving to a new backbone throws that away. SDXL-derived anime bases still dominate that community.

Misconceptions

  • "Transformers beat U-Nets." The evidence is confounded by latent vs pixel space and by training recipes. EDM2 (a U-Net) and SiD2 (U-ViT-ish) are competitive, and SD3's own comparison has UViT > DiT.
  • "DiTs have no locality bias." Patchify, RoPE, Mix-FFN convolutions and local windows all add it back.
  • "Lower FID means a better architecture." Many claims rest on 0.1–0.3 FID gaps under different guidance methods (CFG vs interval CFG vs AutoGuidance). Inception features also misjudge modern text-to-image (CMMD).
  • "Flux's guidance_scale is CFG." It is not. On the dev model it is a distilled guidance embedding fed into vec: one forward pass instead of two.
