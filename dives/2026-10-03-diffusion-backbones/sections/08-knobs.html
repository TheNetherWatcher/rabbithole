Competing approaches

  • U-ViT (Bao 2023): everything is a token (t, c, patches), with long skips joining block i to block L−i. Scores 2.29 on ImageNet-256, against DiT's 2.27. It showed the long skip, not up/downsampling, is the U-Net's essential part.
  • EDM2 (Karras 2024): stays a U-Net but makes every layer magnitude-preserving, with forced unit-norm weights, no GroupNorm, and SiLU divided by 0.596. Gets ImageNet-512 FID 2.41 → 1.81 at equal complexity, and matches the previous state of the art at 5× smaller size. This is the strongest "U-Nets were never the problem" result.
  • SiD2 / U-ViT pixel (Hoogeboom 2024): FID 1.5 at 512 in pixel space. Spending compute on resolution (smaller patches) beats doubling channels.
  • SSMs (DiS, ZigMa, DiM, DiG): linear in T. They win on memory at ≥1K resolution (DiG-S/2 is 2.5× faster than DiT-S/2 at 1792² and uses 75.7% less memory; DiG). They never led on FID, and no frontier model uses a pure SSM.
  • Linear attention + deep compression (SANA): 0.6B parameters, claimed 100× higher throughput than Flux-12B. Production-grade for images, but SANA-Video 2.0 went hybrid, with about 25% softmax layers (2607.21553).
  • Autoregressive (Emu3, VAR, Janus) and AR + diffusion head (MAR, Transfusion, BAGEL): see §11.
