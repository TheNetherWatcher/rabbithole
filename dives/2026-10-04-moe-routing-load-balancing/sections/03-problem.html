  • Compute per layer is gated by the max expert load under EP, not the mean. With a mean of 500 and a max of 1,750 (my toy run below: MaxVio ≈ 2.5), the layer takes about 3.5× the balanced time.
  • Capacity caps turn imbalance into waste. Switch uses CF 1.0–2.0; ST-MoE uses 1.25 in training and 2.0 in evaluation. Overflow becomes dropped tokens (a quality loss) or padding (wasted FLOPs).
  • Unbalanced training starves experts of gradient, so the extra parameters are dead weight.
