• The balance versus specialization Pareto frontier has no agreed shape. Batch-wise loosening helps (Qiu), and loss-free matches or beats aux loss (Wang), yet strict balance at inference is still wanted for EP.
  • Training and serving mismatch: the bias controller exists only in training. At serve time, batch makeup decides the load. Decoupling routing from batch composition is unsolved, and it is also the security issue.
  • Learnability of routers: linear routers with top-k have non-differentiable selection. ReLU, Dirichlet and OT routing trade stability for gradient. There is no sample-complexity theory.
  • Optimal granularity beyond small scale.
  • Why the obvious fixes fail: a bigger α kills specialization; per-sequence balance forces uniformity where the data isn’t uniform; expert choice gives balance but breaks batch independence.
