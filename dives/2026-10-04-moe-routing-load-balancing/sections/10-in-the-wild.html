• DeepSeek-V3: bias γ = 0.001, then 0.0; sigmoid with normalisation; node-limited to M = 4; a tiny sequence-wise loss (α = 1e-4) as a safety net.
  • Mixtral and the Megatron default: Switch-style aux loss with a coefficient of about 1e-2, and a softmax over the top-k logits.
  • Megablocks: reformulates the experts as one block-sparse matmul, so imbalance costs no extra FLOPs. No drops.
  • vLLM and SGLang serving: moe_align_block_size sorts token-expert pairs and pads them to block multiples before the grouped GEMM.
  • OLMoE: dropless token-choice routing. It reports early routing saturation and low expert co-activation.
