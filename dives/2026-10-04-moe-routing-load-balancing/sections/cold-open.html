Under expert parallelism, a layer waits for its busiest expert.

A Mixture-of-Experts router sends each token to its top 2 of 4 experts. Left alone, it piles traffic onto a few favourites, and one hot expert holds up the whole layer.

Balanced: 500 tokens per expert.
Busiest expert: 1,750 tokens, so the layer takes 3.5× as long.
