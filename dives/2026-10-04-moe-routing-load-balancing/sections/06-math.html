Switch/GShard aux loss

L_aux = α · N · Σ_i f_i · P_i, with f_i = (1/T) Σ_x 1[argmax p(x) = i] (hard fraction) and P_i = (1/T) Σ_x p_i(x) (soft probability mass). N = experts, T = tokens, α = 1e-2 in Switch. → in plain terms: how much of the traffic goes to expert i, times how much the router is leaning toward it, summed over experts.
  • Under uniform routing, f_i = P_i = 1/N, so L = αN · N · (1/N²) = α. By a Cauchy–Schwarz-type rearrangement, this is the minimum for a fixed Σ P_i = 1.
  • Why a product, not f² or P²? f_i is non-differentiable (argmax). Using f_i · P_i lets the gradient flow through P_i, weighted by how loaded expert i actually is: ∂L/∂p_i(x) = α N f_i / T. Popular experts get pushed down in proportion, and the loss stays differentiable.

→ in plain terms: the penalty pushes the router’s probability down on experts in proportion to how overloaded they are.

Router z-loss (ST-MoE)

L_z = (1/B) Σ_x (log Σ_j e^{x_j})², c_z = 1e-3. → in plain terms: penalises large router logits, to keep the softmax numerically sane.

Loss-free balancing (Wang et al. 2024; DeepSeek-V3)

Selection: s_i + b_i ∈ TopK(·). Gate: from the unbiased s_i. Update: b_i ← b_i + u · sign(c̄ − c_i), where c_i is the number of tokens routed to expert i in the batch and c̄ is the mean. → in plain terms: an integral-style controller on load.

Reported u = 0.001 (Wang) and γ = 0.001 for the first 14.3T tokens, then 0.0 for the last 500B (V3).

Why sign and not a proportional update? The step size is independent of how large the violation is, so one wildly hot expert does not make the bias overshoot. The cost is a limit cycle with amplitude about u in score space. In the demo below, a u that is too big gives permanent jitter.

Why there is no gradient interference. ∂L_LM/∂W sees only the LM objective, and b has no dependence on W through autograd. The aux-loss approach adds α N f_i ∂P_i/∂W to the same W: if α is too small, the router collapses; if α is too large, the router logits are pulled toward uniform regardless of input, erasing specialization.

V3’s complement. A sequence-wise balance loss with α = 0.0001 remains, “just to avoid extreme imbalance within any single sequence”.
