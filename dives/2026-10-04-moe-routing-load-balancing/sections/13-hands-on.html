loss_free_demo.py (in this folder, numpy only): a frozen skewed router, 16 experts, top-2, T = 4096 tokens per step, 400 steps. Real output from my run:

u=0.0    MaxVio step0=2.523  step100=2.580  mean(last100)=2.568  |b|max=0.000
u=0.001  MaxVio step0=2.523  step100=1.217  mean(last100)=0.084  |b|max=0.293
u=0.01   MaxVio step0=2.523  step100=0.098  mean(last100)=0.158  |b|max=0.360
u=0.05   MaxVio step0=2.523  step100=0.512  mean(last100)=0.492  |b|max=0.500

Reading it. u = 0 never balances. u = 0.001 converges slowly but settles tight. u = 0.01 converges fast but jitters. u = 0.05 limit-cycles and never settles. Wang et al. report the same shape (0.001 best, 0.01 fluctuates).

Caveat: this is a toy with a frozen router and no LM loss. It shows the controller, not model quality.

Try: add a “quality” term where load raises an expert’s score, to watch the collapse feedback. Or swap sign for a proportional update.
